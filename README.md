## 💡 What is Wallink?

Traditional messaging hides your conversations behind notifications and app inboxes. **Wallink** changes how you connect with your partner, best friend, or loved one by bringing your shared space directly to where you look hundreds of times a day — **your phone's wallpaper**.

Whether you want to leave an affectionate morning note, sketch a live doodle together, or see their mood change in real-time, Wallink makes presence effortless and passive.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🖼️ 1. Two Distinct Connection Modes

| Feature | 🎨 Personal Wallpaper Mode | 🤝 Shared Canvas Mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | **Your own choice** (preset, gallery photo, or system default) | **Shared identical canvas** chosen together |
| **Sync Philosophy** | "Different backgrounds, shared words" | "Our single joint universe" |
| **Content** | Real-time dynamic text notes, stickers, subtle badges | Full collaborative drawings, doodles, notes, and photos |
| **Best For** | Keeping your aesthetic while receiving sweet notes | Long-distance pairs wanting a collaborative drawing space |

### ⚡ 2. Instant Live Wallpaper Updates
- **Android Native Engine:** Powered by Android's `WallpaperService.Engine` for zero battery drain and smooth 60fps canvas composition.
- **Glanceable Presence:** The moment your partner writes or updates a sticky note, your home screen and lock screen update automatically upon unlock.

### 🔐 3. Private & Secure by Design
- Simple 6-digit pair codes and QR code pairing.
- End-to-end secure transmission of pair canvas events.
- Your personal wallpapers and photos stay strictly between you two.

## 🔄 How It Works

```
[Device A: Partner 1] ─── (Draw / Leave Note) ───► [Secure Celora Sync]
                                                            │
                                                            ▼
[Device B: Partner 2] ◄─── (Live Wallpaper Redraw) ─────────┘
```

1. **Pair Up:** Generate an invite code or scan your partner's QR code.
2. **Choose Mode:** Select whether you want your own background with shared notes or a shared collaborative canvas.
3. **Set Wallpaper:** Grant Live Wallpaper permission once.
4. **Stay Connected:** Send notes, doodles, and reactions anytime.
